# Roster Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** October 31, 2026

## 1. Overview

Roster is a Discord bot designed to help server administrators manage their staff teams, track activity, and moderate their communities.

This Privacy Policy explains what information Roster collects, how it is used, and how users can request access to or deletion of their information.

## 2. Information We Collect

Roster collects information necessary to provide its features.

Depending on which features a server uses, this may include:

- Discord user IDs and server IDs
- Discord usernames and display names
- Staff roles and staff membership records
- Staff applications and application responses
- Staff activity and performance information
- Warnings and disciplinary records
- Moderation actions and their associated reasons
- Promotion, demotion, and other staff management records
- Information submitted through commands, forms, and interactions
- Message information required for certain moderation and logging features

The specific information collected depends on the features used by each server.

## 3. Messages and Server Activity

Roster primarily operates through Discord slash commands and interactions.

Roster does not continuously collect or permanently archive general server message history.

However, certain features require temporary access to message content:

- **Snipe:** When a message is deleted, Roster temporarily retains information about that message so it can be retrieved using the `/snipe` command. Roster keeps the most recently deleted message across the entire server. When another message is deleted, the previous snipe entry is replaced.
- **Message Logging:** If a server administrator enables message logging, Roster may process and send relevant message information to the server's configured logging channel. Depending on the logging features enabled, this may include message content, author information, timestamps, and other relevant details.

Information processed by these features is used to provide their intended functionality.

Outside of these features, Roster does not intentionally archive ordinary server conversations.

## 4. How Information Is Used

Collected information is used to:

- Provide staff management and moderation features
- Maintain staff records and histories
- Track staff activity and performance
- Process applications and staff requests
- Maintain warnings and disciplinary records
- Generate statistics and reports
- Provide message logging and snipe functionality
- Maintain the security and reliability of Roster
- Troubleshoot issues and improve the Bot

We do not intentionally collect information unrelated to Roster's functionality.

## 5. Data Storage

Roster stores information in a database to provide its features.

Stored information is limited to what is necessary for the Bot's functionality. Access to stored information is restricted to authorized individuals responsible for operating and maintaining Roster.

Some features, such as `/snipe`, use temporary storage rather than maintaining a permanent message archive.

## 6. Data Sharing

We do not sell users' personal information.

Information may be processed by Discord and infrastructure providers necessary to operate Roster.

Information collected through message logging may be sent to logging channels configured by the server administrator.

We may also disclose information when required by law or when reasonably necessary to protect the security of Roster and its users.

We do not intentionally share stored information for advertising purposes.

## 7. Data Retention

Information is retained for as long as reasonably necessary to provide Roster's features and maintain relevant staff and moderation records.

Temporary information used by `/snipe` is replaced when another message is deleted across the server.

Other records, including staff information, warnings, and moderation logs, may remain stored until they are deleted by an authorized server administrator or through an approved data deletion request.

## 8. Your Choices and Data Deletion

Users and server administrators may request access to or deletion of information associated with them or their server.

To make a request, contact:

**DiscordRosterSupport@gmail.com**

Please include the relevant Discord user ID or server ID and describe the information you would like to access or delete.

Requests will be reviewed and handled within a reasonable period, subject to applicable legal requirements.

## 9. Security

We take reasonable measures to protect stored information against unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or destruction.

However, no online service or storage system can guarantee complete security.

## 10. Children's Privacy

Roster is not intentionally designed to collect personal information from children below the minimum age required to use Discord under applicable rules and laws.

## 11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as Roster develops.

Changes will be published on this page with an updated revision date.

## 12. Contact

For questions about this Privacy Policy or requests concerning your information, contact:
